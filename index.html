ALCPokeGrading Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

Summary: ALCPokeGrading is designed to help users estimate the condition of collectible cards from images they choose to provide. We do not sell personal information or card images.

1. Information You Provide

ALCPokeGrading may process photographs or images of collectible cards that you choose to capture or select for grading analysis. The app may also create grading results, scores, notes, and related card-history information based on those images.

2. Camera and Photo Library Access

If you grant permission, ALCPokeGrading may access your device camera or photo library so that you can capture or select card images. The app only accesses images you choose to provide through the app.

3. How Card Images Are Used

Card images may be processed to evaluate visible characteristics such as centering, corners, edges, surface condition, whitening, and other condition-related features. When AI-based analysis is used, the selected images and related request data may be securely transmitted to a third-party AI service solely to provide the requested analysis.

4. Storage of Grading Results

ALCPokeGrading may store grading results and card-history information so that you can review previously analyzed cards. Depending on the version and features of the app, this information may be stored on your device or in app-related storage used to provide the feature.

5. Information We Do Not Sell

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, card images, or grading results to advertisers or data brokers.

6. Analytics and Diagnostics

Apple or other service providers may provide limited diagnostic or crash information when permitted by your device settings. Such information is used to maintain and improve app reliability.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally in the app may be removed by deleting the applicable saved record or by deleting the app, depending on the feature. If a future version of ALCPokeGrading provides account-based or cloud storage, this policy will be updated to explain applicable retention and deletion options.

8. Children's Privacy

ALCPokeGrading is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users should not submit images containing personal or sensitive information.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical measures designed to protect information processed by the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

11. Contact
